# Global Superstore Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive four-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales,
profitability, customer segments, regional performance, and shipping operations.

The project transforms the Global Superstore dataset into business insights
that support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence
- Global Superstore Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Sales and Profit Trends
- Sales Contribution by Category
- Sales by Market

### 2. Product and Profitability
- Discount vs. Profit Analysis
- Sales by Category
- Top Products by Sales
- Profit Margin by Region and Category

### 3. Customer and Market
- Top 10 Customers
- Region and Category Sales Matrix
- Customer and Market Performance
- Sales by Customer Segment

### 4. Shipping and Operations
- Orders by Priority
- Shipping Cost vs. Sales by Ship Mode
- Shipping Delay by Market
- Average Shipping Delay by Ship Mode

## Key Business Questions
- How are sales and profits changing over time?
- Which product categories contribute the most sales?
- How are discounts associated with profitability?
- Which customers generate the highest sales?
- Which markets and regions contribute the most revenue?
- How do shipping methods affect shipping costs and delays?

## Key Findings
The dashboard is designed to help investigate sales performance,
product profitability, customer contributions, regional differences,
and shipping efficiency.

Specific findings should be validated against the underlying data
and dashboard calculations.

## How to Use
1. Download the Power BI project file.
2. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Explore the four report pages.
4. Use the slicers to investigate different years, categories,
   customer segments, and markets.
